The original Pilkwang and FOCUS3D weights are frozen. The rich arms use FOCUS3D mask-derived features; the permuted arm destroys their within-video association.

Paired event audit (the same 31 events are used by every arm): rich logistic regression beats the 3-feature logistic baseline on 5 events, ties on 24, and loses on 2; the top-1 difference is +3/31 (+9.7 percentage points). Against the fixed geometry baseline the difference is +4/31 (+12.9 percentage points). The video-cluster bootstrap 95% interval for the rich-minus-3-feature event difference is [-0.0417, 0.3750] events per video, so this panel does not establish a reliable generalization gain.

The rich arm versus the existing jointly permuted arm differs by +7 top-1 events (8 wins, 22 ties, 1 loss). This is suggestive that the FOCUS-derived columns carry signal, but it is not a clean mask-only control: `MASK_FEATURES` also permutes daughter separation and source-composition counts (`focus_instance_count`, `consensus_count`), while missing values are imputed fold-wise. Therefore the permutation result cannot be attributed to contour geometry alone.

In the 1,967 union candidates, all 31 positive candidates are present. The FOCUS-derived columns are frequently unavailable: volume balance is missing for 22.9%, parent/daughter union features and volume conservation for 32.7%, and mean FOCUS quality for 1.2%. Missingness is slightly lower for positives than negatives, so the learned gain may partly reflect availability/source composition rather than mask shape itself.
